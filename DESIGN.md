## Brand & Style

### Personality & Values
The brand is energetic, approachable, hard-working, and unapologetically retail-driven. It captures the vibrancy of the traditional Peruvian grocery sector (bodegas, mercados, minimarkets) combined with modern B2B supply-chain efficiency. Tone is commercial, direct, trustworthy, and solution-focused: "Tu surtido completo al mejor precio por mayor, directo a tu negocio".

### Target Audience
Bodegueros, dueños de puestos de abarrotes, minimarkets y pequeños comerciantes independientes. Users prioritize clear margins, volume discounts, reliable next-day delivery, fast ordering from mobile devices, and familiar retail cues over sterile corporate minimalism.

### Aesthetic Movement
**High-Impact Retail Commercial:** A dense, expressive, high-contrast visual system inspired by retail circulars and modern omnichannel supermarkets. It uses saturated promotional banners, high-contrast offer chips, explicit wholesale price comparisons ("x mayor vs sugerido"), vibrant category pills, and tactile elevation to celebrate volume, speed, and real savings.

## Colors

### Color Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#F59E0B` / `#FFB800`):** The engine of commercial warmth and confidence. Used for primary interactive highlights, top header utility banners, deal highlight badges, and high-visibility star ratings.
- **Secondary (`#DC2626` / `#E11D48`):** The promotional accelerator. Used for conversion-critical CTAs ("Descargar App", "Regístrate Gratis"), strike-through retail price anchors, "Ofertón Bodeguero" flashes, and limited-time stock tags.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A`):** Trust, fresh categories, and margin gains. Applied to margin badges ("Gana hasta 28%"), verified merchant checkmarks, logistics guarantees ("Despacho gratis en 24h"), and fresh-produce tags.
- **Warm Accent / Secondary Push (`#EA580C`):** Used on secondary category borders, wholesale threshold badges ("A partir de 3 fardos"), and app download pill buttons.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFDF7` / `#FFF7ED`):** Warm off-white foundation avoiding cold digital blues. Cards sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with borders tinted in `#FDE68A` or `#F3F4F6`.
- **Text & Foreground:** Deep charcoal-brown (`#1C1917`) for primary copy ensures WCAG AAA readability across warm background washes; muted text uses `#57534E`.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headings (Epilogue):** Chosen for its punchy, structural character. At 800 and 700 weights, it channels the direct authority of supermarket flyers and street signage while maintaining digital precision.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances technical clarity with friendly, approachable geometry. Highly legible at small dimensions inside wholesale item matrices, table rows, and margin calculators.

### Commercial Hierarchy Rules
1. **Wholesale Pricing:** Uses dedicated sizing (`price-hero`) in bold red or deep charcoal, paired with strikethrough retail reference prices in smaller, muted typography.
2. **Promotional Badges:** Text in `label-sm` or `label-md` must be rendered in ALL CAPS with bold weights for immediate scanning (e.g., "PACK AHORRO", "EXCLUSIVO APP").

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Density Rules
The page follows a flexible 12-column grid on desktop (max width: 1280px) and a 4-column flow on mobile. Unlike sparse SaaS layouts, spacing is intentionally structured to simulate a well-stocked catalog shelf:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Gutter of 16px (`1rem`), side margins of 16px. Category shortcuts run in a horizontal swipeable rail with `space-sm` gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Gutter of 24px (`1.5rem`), side margins of 32px (`2rem`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Gutter of 32px (`2rem`), margins centered within a 1240px container.

### Element Spacing Rhythms
- Product cards and offer modules utilize compact padding (`space-md`) to ensure critical commercial data (image, brand, wholesale tier, unit margin, CTA) remains visible above the fold on mobile devices.
- Section vertical dividers use `space-xl` with warm tonal band shifts (`#FFFDF7` to `#FFF7ED`) to guide users between hero value props, catalog previews, testimonials, and app store download links.

## Elevation & Depth

### Visual Depth Model
The system uses an **Ambient Warm Shadow & Tonal Layer** approach:
- **Level 1 (Card & Category Pills):** Subtle 1px warm border (`#FED7AA` or `#E5E7EB`) backed by a warm ambient drop shadow (`0 2px 8px -1px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Promo Cards & Testimonial Slates):** Distinct lift (`0 8px 20px -3px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.12)`) reinforced with a top or corner promotional tab.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar / Download Modal):** High-contrast sheet elevated with `0 -6px 24px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`, prioritizing mobile app installation CTAs.
- **Badges & Tags:** Flat with solid contrast backgrounds; no drop shadow, relying on vibrant fill against clean cards for raw scanning speed.

## Shapes

### Form Language
The visual system leverages `roundedness: 2` (base radius of `0.5rem` / 8px):
- **Cards & Category Tiles:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), delivering a modern retail feel without becoming childishly circular.
- **Buttons & App Store CTAs:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) to `0.75rem` for grounded, tactile action items.
- **Promotional Badges & Quantity Selectors:** Pill shapes (`9999px`) for discount chips ("-15%", "OFERTA PACK") to contrast strongly against rectangular product containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA ("Descargar App", "Crear Cuenta"):** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold weight. Subtle hover shift to `#B91C1C` with small translateY(-1px) tactile movement.
- **Wholesale Action CTA ("Pedir por WhatsApp / App"):** Background `#16A34A`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon prefixed.
- **Secondary Retail Button ("Ver Catálogo Completo"):** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#1C1917`, bold weight.

### Promotional Badges & Price Chips
- **Discount Badge:** Solid `#DC2626` background, white uppercase text, positioned on the top-left of offer cards.
- **Wholesale Margin Chip:** Solid `#DCFCE7` background, text `#15803D`, displaying estimated bodega profit (e.g., "Margen 24%").
- **Volume Tier Label:** Bordered tag with `#FEF3C7` background, text `#92400E` ("Desde 5 planchas").

### Wholesale Product Cards
- Contained within a white box with an amber-tinted border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Header:** Category tag + brand logo/name in `label-sm`.
- **Center:** Crisp product imagery on pure white background.
- **Pricing Matrix:** Primary wholesale price in `price-hero` font (`#1C1917` or `#DC2626`), crossed-out regular supermarket price next to it, and bold unit-cost breakdown below.
- **Footer:** Fast add-to-order stepper or direct "Pedir en App" button.

### Category Visual Rails
- Compact circular or rounded-square containers (`#FFF7ED`) with clear iconography or photo illustration (Bebidas, Lácteos, Abarrotes, Limpieza, Golosinas).
- Active item states highlight with an amber outline (`#F59E0B`) and filled background.

### Testimonial Cards ("Voz del Bodeguero")
- Warm card background (`#FFFFFF`) with a top quote badge.
- Includes photo of the bodega owner, bodega name ("Bodega Don Lucho - Comas"), and a verified metric badge ("Ahorro mensual promedio: S/ 850").

### Sticky Mobile App Utility Bar
- Fixed to viewport bottom on screens < 768px.
- Displays app rating stars (4.8 ★), quick promo text ("Envío gratis en tu 1er pedido"), and direct Google Play / App Store tap targets.
